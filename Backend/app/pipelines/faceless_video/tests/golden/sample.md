# The Quantum Computing Paradigm

## Executive Summary
Quantum computing replaces classical binary bits with quantum bits or qubits. This shift enables processing complex states exponentially faster than classical supercomputers for specific problem classes.

- Qubits utilize superposition to exist as 0 and 1 simultaneously.
- Quantum entanglement links qubit states instantaneously across physical distance.
- Modern quantum systems require ultra-low temperatures near absolute zero (-273.15°C).
